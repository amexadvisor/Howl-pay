AdsGram Reward Block

Ad N in a cycle pays Start − Reduction × (N − 1). After the last ad of a cycle the block locks for the cooldown. Ads per cycle 0 turns it off. "No click pays" is the base % paid when the view completes (0 clicks). Clicking in-ad buttons awards tiered bonuses: when 3 ads appear, 1 click pays the 1-Click tier %, 2 clicks pay the 2-Clicks tier %, and 3 clicks pay 100%. When fewer ads appear, tiers adapt automatically. Changes apply to everyone immediately.
